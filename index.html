The Four Tones of Mandarin

Pitch is shown on a relative 5-level scale (Chao 1948): 1 = lowest, 5 = highest. Click any tone to hear an example. Contours: 1 high level (5–5), 2 rising (3–5), 3 dipping (2–1–4), 4 falling (5–1). The neutral tone is short and unstressed; its pitch shifts depending on the previous tone (often around level 2 after tone 1, lower after tone 4).
