## Notes

- **Removed from original list:** `néng (能)` — no productive tone-pair partner exists in standard Mandarin (the syllable `neng` only occurs as 2nd tone). Original entry incorrectly paired it with 弄 (`nòng`, not `nèng`).
- **Removed from original list:** within-character heteronym `jǐ/jī (几/几)` — both sides showed the same character. Replaced with 几/鸡.
- **Frequency caveat:** this is a curated list of high-frequency monosyllabic *content* words paired for clean tone contrast. Strict top-50 by raw corpus frequency would include grammatical particles (的, 了, 着, 地, 们) which are mostly neutral tone and thus poor for tone-contrast practice.
